# awesome-Device-Detox
Awesome resources and apps for device detox and time restrictions.

### Android and iOS
 - [AppBlock](https://appblock.app/)
 - [Ripple](https://ripplefocus.github.io/en/) - Free local-only Android and iOS 16+ app blocker with parental unlock codes and anti-uninstall. Blocks short-video feeds inside WeChat, YouTube, and Instagram while keeping chats usable.

### iOS
 - [minulo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6809352229) - Hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok For You while DMs keep working. Can lock the official apps with Screen Time. Hiding is free; nightly schedules and Hard mode are Pro.

### Android
 - [AppDetox (removed from Play store)](https://appdetox.github.io/about/)
 - [Digital Detox](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.urbandroid.ddc)
